Our Partner Brands

Step into a world of captivating light with Telsa Lighting's collection of distinguished brands. These brands redefine illumination, turning mere spaces into breathtaking experiences that resonate with sophistication and style.

Plusrite

The Plusrite® brand of products focuses on traditional lighting solutions like high intensity discharge (HIDs), halogens, linear fluorescents, compact fluorescent light bulbs (CFLs), ballasts and germicidal lighting solutions. In fact, Plusrite® is the largest producer of HIDs and metal halide products in the word and white label products for most of the top OEMs.

Naturaled

NaturaLED® has been thoughtfully designed for residential and commercial projects for both indoor and outdoor LED applications like retail spaces, offices, warehouses, manufacturing, and industrial locations. With warehouses located in California, Texas, North Carolina, and Canada, the NaturaLED® brand stocks products to serve North American customers quickly and efficiently.

Ecosysteams

Designed and engineered for the retail and consumer market, the Ecostreams® brand is comprised of high-quality products that utilize function and form. Smart, economical LED products that make life easier, from compact and portable work-lights to barn lights and security lights. Ecostreams® provides a go-to solution for the on-the-go community.

The MillWright

Naturaled® is excited to showcase The MillWright industrial, round high bay, which is custom engineered to thrive in dusty, high-heat environments like steel mills. The MillWright combines the finest characteristics of high-quality LED lighting into a high-performance fixture that endures both water and sizeable vibrations.

The Global Electric  and Industrial Products

The Global Electric and Industrial Products brand encompasses a wide range of electrical and industrial products that support our business partners. We design and manufacture products like weatherproof boxes, covers, accessories, conduits, fittings, straps and hangars, motor controls and more.
