What We Offer

Tesla Lighting specializes in outdoor and indoor commercial lighting products, consistently exceeding expectations in intricate architectural projects and expansive commercial spaces. With our expertise and attention to detail, we create lighting solutions that highlight the unique features of architectural designs, adding captivating visual effects while seamlessly integrating with the overall aesthetics. In expansive commercial spaces such as retail stores, office buildings, and hospitality venues, we understand the importance of creating a welcoming and productive environment.

Our Services

At Telsa Lighting, our role is to coordinate and assemble teams for projects. We connect with skilled electricians and contractors and partner with different experts to ensure the job is completed successfully. We aim to bring the right people together to create personalized and expert results.

Light Expertise

We offer various services to meet diverse lighting needs. These include lighting design, distribution of lighting products, energy analysis to determine cost savings and lighting consulting.

Light Analysis

For clients seeking advice on lighting solutions, we provide expert guidance. For example, suppose a customer has a warehouse with lighting challenges and wants to improve it. In that case, we conduct a lighting audit or survey to assess the existing setup and recommend the best solution for their specific space or environment. We aim to provide comprehensive support and deliver optimal lighting solutions for our clients.

Light Design

Lighting design strategically plans and creates lighting schemes for spaces, considering Conforming Requirements, High-Performance Ability, Responsibility & Sustainability & Climate Ambition.

Light Guarantee

With 20 years of experience, we offer lighting layout services, highlighting and solving pain points. Our expertise and knowledge ensure client education throughout the process. We prioritize quality solutions and welcome partnerships, regardless of project size. Our offerings include warranties of 5 to 10 years, depending on the lighting product.
